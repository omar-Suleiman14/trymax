Nine primitives. Everything else is configuration.

Max has no built-in idea of a phone, a customer or a wallet. It has items, people, accounts and transactions, and a blueprint that turns them into the shop you run.

Item

Anything the shop stocks, sells, repairs or lends.

Person

Customers, suppliers, staff. Whoever a transaction involves.

Account

Where money sits: the drawer, a wallet, a bank, a debt.

Transaction

Goods and money moving, recorded with who did it and when.

Property

The typed fields you define. IMEI is a property, not a feature.

Template

A saved starting point for the records you create all day.

View

A filtered, sorted, grouped slice of a database.

Page

A document that holds text and databases side by side.

Blueprint

The whole configuration as one file you export and import.

No account. No server. No subscription.

Max is a desktop application with a local database. Nothing is uploaded, nothing phones home, and every screen keeps working with the network unplugged.

Local SQLite, authoritative
WAL · integrity check at startup
Arabic and English
RTL and LTR, both first class
Reversals, not deletions
a mistake becomes a counter-record
Backups you hold
safety snapshot before every restore
Quick actions
Ctrl S from anywhere in the app
Search the whole workspace
Ctrl F across pages and records
Cloud backup is opt-in, never required.
Your data stays a file you can copy.
Windows, macOS and Linux builds.

One form records the sale, the stock and the money.

A quick action is a form you define yourself. Filling it once writes the transaction, moves the stock and posts the money to the right account. Required fields come first; optional ones stay folded away until you ask for them.

  • Required fields first, optional fields collapsed
  • Last used values remembered per action
  • Reversals leave the original record in place
  • Any view exports to Excel
